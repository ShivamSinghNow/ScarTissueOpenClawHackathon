⚠️ **ScarTissue Warning**: This PR reintroduces the bug fixed in commit 2bc982b73c58d8dac8f77a7e3bc339a6cf672685. The removed serialization logic is not redundant—it is required to convert `reasoning_content` from `additional_kwargs` back to Ollama's `thinking` field format. Without this mapping, multi-turn conversations with reasoning models like DeepSeek-R1 will silently drop the chain-of-thought content when messages are serialized back to Ollama's wire format, breaking agents that depend on preserving prior reasoning across conversation turns.
